# Disclaimer

Muskometer is an **entertainment** menu bar utility. It is **not** financial advice, investment guidance, tax advice, or a trading tool.

## What the numbers mean

- **Paper gains** are calculated as `share count × (current price − prior close)`.
- Figures are **illustrative** — they reflect reported holdings and live market prices, not realized wealth or cash flows.
- Quotes may be delayed, stale, or wrong. SEC filings may lag actual ownership.
- The **market-cap parity** card (“If Tesla had SpaceX’s market cap”) is a toy implied TSLA price under equal *company* market caps. It is **not** a merger announcement, fairness opinion, deal model, or investment thesis.

## TSLA and SPCX

- **TSLA / SPCX ownership** — Musk’s share counts from SEC Form 4 filings; prices from Yahoo Finance.
- **Issuer outstanding** (parity card only) — company-wide shares outstanding from SEC companyfacts or cover-derived defaults, separate from Form 4 ownership.

See [HOLDINGS.md](HOLDINGS.md) for how holdings, outstanding, and quotes combine.

## Holdings data

Ownership share counts come from **public SEC Form 4 filings** attributed to Elon Musk. You can override ownership counts in Settings; defaults follow the latest SEC sync. Issuer outstanding for the parity card is synced best-effort on the same cadence and is independent of Form 4.
